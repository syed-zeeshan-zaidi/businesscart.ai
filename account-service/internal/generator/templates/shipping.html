Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive an order confirmation by email after your purchase is complete. If there are any issues with your order, we will contact you directly.

Returns & Exchanges

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, please contact us to discuss a return or exchange. We handle returns on a case-by-case basis and will work with you to find a fair resolution. Please reach out within a reasonable timeframe after receiving your order.
